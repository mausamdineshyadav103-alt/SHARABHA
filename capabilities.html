WHAT WE'RE WORKING ON

Five connected domains. One engineering stack.

Sharabha is currently developing capabilities across five interconnected technology domains. Each project stands on its own — and each strengthens the others as we move from design to physical validation.

DESIGN COMPLETE • TESTING PHASE
01

AEROSPACE PROPULSION

Jet & Turboprop Propulsion

At the core of Sharabha's propulsion vertical is a next-generation turboprop engine platform. The engine design is complete and currently in its testing phase, built to run on hydrogen-compatible combustion systems alongside conventional fuels.

  • Compressor and turbine design — engineered for aerodynamic efficiency and domestic manufacturability using Indian materials.
  • Combustion chamber engineering — built to accommodate hydrogen combustion alongside standard aviation fuels.
  • Materials and thermal engineering — addressing extreme temperatures on combustor liners, injectors, and hot-section alloys.
  • Instrumented test stand validation — operating under controlled conditions ahead of full-scale production.
ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
02

CLEAN ENERGY PRODUCTION

Green Hydrogen Production

Clean hydrogen sits at the center of Sharabha's technology stack. We are actively developing PEM electrolysis systems to produce clean hydrogen at scale, working toward establishing a green hydrogen plant as a foundational domestic energy asset.

  • PEM electrolysis systems — using proton exchange membrane technology to split water into hydrogen and oxygen cleanly using renewable electricity.
  • Green hydrogen plant vision — a long-term infrastructure project designed to anchor domestic clean fuel production for India's future.
  • Electrode & catalyst research — membrane electrode assemblies, catalyst coatings, and durable bipolar plates engineered for domestic fabrication.
  • Scalable system architecture — designed to expand from laboratory units to commercial production for propulsion and industrial demand.
SYSTEMS RESEARCH
03

ENERGY SYSTEMS & STORAGE

Electricity & Power Systems

Reliable, resilient power generation underpins everything else we build. Our energy systems team investigates distributed generation, renewable integration, and hydrogen-linked storage to support a more sovereign national grid.

  • Grid integration research — exploring methods to seamlessly integrate variable renewable sources with high stability.
  • Hydrogen-linked energy storage — investigating hydrogen as a long-duration storage and transmission medium for electricity.
  • Generators & power electronics — in development for both stationary grid-connected sites and mobile field installations.
  • Decentralized resilience — reducing reliance on imported substation and power conversion technology.
SOVEREIGN DEFENSE ARCHITECTURE
04

DEFENSE & SECURITY

Defense Applications

Sharabha's propulsion and energy systems are developed with defense-grade reliability as a primary requirement from day one — including new design work dedicated to modern air defense systems.

  • New air defense designs — in active development, aimed at strengthening indigenous air defense capability with secure, field-ready engineering.
  • Indigenous propulsion platforms — replacing imported engines with locally manufactured and serviced propulsion units.
  • Field-deployable power systems — compact, robust hydrogen and electrical platforms engineered for extreme field conditions.
  • Strategic alignment — pursuing active engagement with DRDO, defense PSUs, and military stakeholders to meet national priorities.
CLEAN MOBILITY
05

HIGH-LOAD MOBILITY

Heavy-Duty Transport

The hydrogen combustion and storage technology engineered for our aviation programs feeds directly into heavy-duty transport — developing hydrogen fuel-cell solutions for high-load, long-distance freight and industrial vehicles.

  • Hydrogen fuel cells — developed to power high-load haulage, converting hydrogen into electricity with zero tailpipe emissions.
  • High-efficiency powertrain integration — adapting aerospace-grade thermodynamic modeling to high-torque vehicle platforms.
  • Fuel delivery & storage architecture — high-pressure, lightweight storage systems engineered for continuous heavy-duty duty cycles.
  • Infrastructure compatibility — designing vehicle fuel interfaces to match emerging national green hydrogen supply corridors.
