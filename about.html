OUR ORIGIN

Building a connected platform, not isolated products.

Sharabha began with a simple but demanding question: why should a country with India's engineering talent, manufacturing base, and strategic ambition still depend on others for the propulsion and energy technologies that power its aircraft, its industry, and its defense forces?

That question became a mission — and today, that mission is a set of active projects. Sharabha was founded to design and build indigenous propulsion and clean-energy systems, and we are currently working across turboprop jet engine development, green hydrogen production, electricity generation research, defense-oriented engineering, and heavy-duty transport applications.

We are not a company chasing a single product. We are building a technology platform, one project at a time — where propulsion engineering, hydrogen production, and power systems research reinforce each other, so that progress in one area accelerates progress in all the others.

OUR APPROACH

Three principles behind our engineering stack.

01

Indigenous by design

From compressor geometry to electrolysis architecture, every system we develop is engineered around what can realistically be sourced, manufactured, and scaled within India — reducing reliance on external suppliers for the technologies that matter most.

02

Cross-disciplinary by necessity

Jet propulsion, hydrogen production, electricity systems, defense engineering, and heavy transport are not isolated projects — they are one connected technology stack. A breakthrough in hydrogen combustion strengthens our propulsion work; advances in electrolysis strengthen our power systems.

03

Rigor before scale

We believe in validating every subsystem — combustion behavior, materials compatibility, electrolysis efficiency — before committing to scale. Our current work moves deliberately from digital design to prototype, test stand validation, and manufacturing readiness.
