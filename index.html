WHY SHARABHA

Building technologies that move nations forward.

The technologies that power aircraft, defense platforms, and heavy industry have historically been imported, licensed, or controlled by a handful of global players. Sharabha exists to change that equation.

We are currently developing indigenous turboprop and jet propulsion systems, hydrogen-compatible combustion architecture, and PEM-based green hydrogen production units — all engineered around Indian manufacturing capability, local supply chains, and strategic needs.

We believe energy independence and defense self-reliance are two sides of the same coin. A nation that produces its own clean fuel, generates its own reliable electricity, and designs its own propulsion platforms is a nation that controls its destiny.
