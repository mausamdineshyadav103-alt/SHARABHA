WHY SHARABHA

Building technologies that move nations forward.

Sharabha is an Indian deep-tech venture building indigenous propulsion and clean-energy systems from the ground up.

The technologies that power aircraft, defense platforms, and heavy industry have historically been imported, licensed, or controlled by a handful of global players. Sharabha exists to change that equation — developing turboprop and jet propulsion systems, hydrogen-compatible combustion architecture, and PEM-based green hydrogen production, all designed around Indian manufacturing capability and strategic needs.

We believe energy independence and defense self-reliance are two sides of the same coin. A nation that can produce its own clean hydrogen, generate its own reliable electricity, and build its own propulsion systems controls its own future.
